Monthly tables

Meal rates and earlier report times change every month. For each month you want to check, add its Meal Allowances PDF (required) and its What’s New bulletin (recommended).

Where to get these PDFs 4 steps
  1. 1

    Open the Block Bid Package

    On the Aeronet portal, go to Crew Scheduling & Planning›Block Bid Package

  2. 2

    Choose the block month, then Base: ALL

    Open the BlockPeriod of the month you want to check, then Base: ALL.

    The two files you need: …_Whats_New-BL.pdf and …-MealAllowances.pdf

  3. 3

    Download both files

    Hover the What’s New file, press the three dots ⋯, then Download. Do the same for the Meal Allowances file.

  4. 4

    Add them here

    Press Add Meal Expenses and Report Time PDFs and choose both files (you can select them together). Repeat for every month you would like to validate your pay.

Check your expenses

Import a Rainmaker Crew Pay Details PDF to confirm every meal allowance and gratuity was paid according to the contract rules.

or drop the PDF here

PDFs are read on this device only. Nothing is uploaded, and the app works without an internet connection (it needs one at least once every 30 days).

How to print the PDF from Rainmaker 6 steps
  1. 1

    Open Crew Pay Details

    In Rainmaker, choose the Period, then press Search.

  2. 2

    Open all the columns

    Press + at the top-left of the table, next to Day. Once pressed it turns into −.

    You should now see the expense columns B, L, D, S and CICO:

  3. 3

    Show the flight details

    Press More ▾, then Details. Each day now lists its flights.

  4. 4

    Print

    Press More ▾ again, then Print.

  5. 5

    Save as PDF, in Landscape

    In the print window, set Printer to Save as PDF and Layout to Landscape, then press Save.

  6. 6

    Import it in this app

    Press Import Rainmaker PDF and choose the saved file. The app first checks that it was printed this way.
